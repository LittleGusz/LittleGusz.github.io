Taruh foto, render SolidWorks, screenshot, dan video project di folder ini.
Biar rapi, bikin satu subfolder per project, contoh:

  assets/img/projects/torque-check/1.jpg
  assets/img/projects/torque-check/2.jpg
  assets/img/projects/torque-check/demo.mp4

Lalu tulis path-nya di js/data.js pada project yang bersangkutan, contoh:

  image: "assets/img/projects/torque-check/1.jpg",
  gallery: [
    "assets/img/projects/torque-check/2.jpg",
    { video: "assets/img/projects/torque-check/demo.mp4",
      poster: "assets/img/projects/torque-check/1.jpg" }
  ]

Nama folder dan file: huruf kecil semua, tanpa spasi (pakai tanda hubung).

Saran foto : JPG atau WebP, lebar sekitar 1200px, di bawah 300 KB.
             Foto sampul (image) paling pas yang landscape.
Saran video: MP4 (H.264), sekitar 720p, usahakan di bawah 20 MB.
